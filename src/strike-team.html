Weekend Strike Team

One planning sheet · One co-op run · Everyone AFKs when they need to

This is the household as a pick-up group: whoever can show up, shows up; nobody rage-quits over dishes. Plan one short co-op run for the weekend (60–90 minutes is usually plenty), assign roles by vibe rather than by age, and keep the pull list honest — three targets beat ten. End with a debrief, hand out XP, and let people go.
